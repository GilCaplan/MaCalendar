Under the Lid

A voice-driven calendar assistant that runs entirely on one host laptop, with thin surfaces attached over a private link — a phone in practice, as many as you like in principle. You speak; an event appears. No account, no API key, nothing sent to any company — and one brain: every command, from every surface, is understood and executed by a single process on the host. Everything else in the drawing is thin.
