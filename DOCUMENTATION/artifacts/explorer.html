Under the Lid

A voice-driven calendar assistant that runs entirely on one laptop, with a phone as a second surface. You speak; an event appears. No account, no API key, nothing sent to any company — and one brain: every command, from either device, is understood and executed by a single process on the laptop. Everything else in the drawing is thin.

3 local models  ·  15 actions  ·  97% on an 89-command corpus  ·  0 requests to the internet

Every framed part opens. The numbered chain is the pipeline — ← → step through it, esc closes, ⌂ returns to the whole system.
